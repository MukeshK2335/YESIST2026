# YESIST12

Edition 2024

Venue


→ View Report

A vibrant morning set the tone for the inauguration of IEEE YESIST12 2024 at Tunis Science City, Tunisia, kicking off an event centered on innovation and global collaboration. The occasion gathered an esteemed lineup of dignitaries and experts, including Dr. Ramalatha Marimuthu, General Chair of IEEE YESIST12, Habib Kammoun, Chair of IEEE Tunisia Section, Andrew Knight, President of IEEE IAS, Eric Berkowitz, Director of Membership at IEEE Computer Society, Dr. Lenin Raja, Director of EMC Test & Training Center Pvt Ltd, and Nehad El-Sherif, Founder of MNKYBR Technologies Inc. The event emphasized the commitment to the IEEE Code of Ethics, followed by the introduction of track and committee chairs. A key highlight was the unveiling of the IEEE YESIST12 Magazine, followed by thought-provoking inaugural addresses from distinguished speakers. As the ceremony concluded, the stage was set for a dynamic competition, with participants poised to present groundbreaking innovations, marking the beginning of the Grand Finale.

# YESIST12

Edition 2022

Venue


→ View Report

The YESIST12 Grand Finale was held as a hybrid event in Sri Venkateshwara College of Engineering, Bangalore from September 10, 2022 to September 11, 2022. The event was live on Microsoft Teams. The international talent show, IEEE YESIST12 was filled with students and young professionals showcasing their fresh ideas to address humanitarian and social issues directly impacting their communities. A year-long journey of identifying and mentoring talent culminated in the Grand Finale with immense participation from finalists, volunteers, and delegates.

# YESIST12

Edition 2021

Venue: Online


→ View Report

IEEE YESIST’12 2021 happened in a virtual mode using the Webex platform due to the global pandemic. The grand finale took place from the 18th to the 20th of September 2021. The final pitching round led to the selection of 6 winners, 8 runner-ups and 6 honourable mentions across 5 tracks including Innovation Challenge, Maker Fair, WePOWER, Junior Einstein and Special Track, involving around 183 participants from 16 countries.
